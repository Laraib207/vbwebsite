Veer Bharat Oil — Category & Highlights

Veer Bharat — Trusted Oils, Thoughtful Practices

Established in 2010, Veer Bharat has grown into a dependable provider of edible oils and allied products, serving households, retailers, and institutional buyers across India. Built on a foundation of quality, transparency, and customer-first service, we combine traditional know-how with modern manufacturing to deliver products that are safe, nutritious, and reliably consistent. Our portfolio includes Kachi Ghani mustard oil, light and nutritious soybean oil, refined palm (palm kernel/pam) oil for high-heat cooking, rice bran oil, sunflower oil, blended cooking oils and specialty formulations. We supply bulk institutional orders as well as retail-packed bottles and tins, meeting the needs of home kitchens, restaurants, and food manufacturers. Each product is developed to suit practical cooking uses — from gentle dressings to high-stability frying — while keeping taste and health in balance.

Why Veer Bharat Mustard Oil

Authentic Kachi Ghani

Traditional cold-pressed goodness

Veer Bharat’s Kachi Ghani mustard oil is crafted using time-honoured cold-pressing methods that preserve the natural aroma, pungency and nutrition of mustard seeds. Sourced from trusted farmers, our cold-pressed oil is minimally processed to retain essential fatty acids, natural antioxidants and a bold, restaurant-quality flavor that elevates everyday cooking.

Double filtration, zero compromise

Purity is non-negotiable — that’s why we maintain rigorous quality checks at every step.
